`frontend_upstream_api_unreachable` (`cert_monitors.tf`) rewritten from an absolute count (`>20 in 10m`, `renotify_interval = 1440`) to a pod-count-invariant **ratio** (`(failed / total) * 100 > 90` over 30m, `require_full_window`, no renotify). The count version flooded ~200 alerts across the 25 orgs on first apply and was destroyed live: the log fires ~24x/minute *per pod*, so a single pod breached `>20 in 10m` in under a minute, and the count scaled with pod count — meaning a crashlooping rollout or an HPA scale-up inflated it fastest, exactly the transients that must not page. The ratio measures failing health checks against total health checks (100% broken vs 0% healthy, measured), so restart storms move numerator and denominator together and do not trigger it.
